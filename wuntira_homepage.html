What We Do

A Business & A Non-Profit — Working As One

01
WAV — The Business

Wuntira Agribusiness Ventures

WAV buys shea nuts, maize, soybeans, sorghum, groundnuts, and more from smallholder farmers at fair, stable prices — then connects them to national and international markets in cosmetics, confectionery, and other industries.

Learn about WAV →
02
Foundation — The Non-Profit

Wuntira Agri Foundation

The Foundation trains women in remote, marginalized communities across northern Ghana on good agricultural practices, climate-smart farming, financial literacy, and forming cooperatives — backed by USAID and Feed the Future GTI.
